# Prompts — Guardião Ossário v1

## Concept de apresentação

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production character concept sheet for a Meta Quest VR game enemy
Primary request: Design the final visual concept for the Ossuary Guardian, replacing the simple procedural blockout while preserving its gameplay silhouette, height, broad torso, humanoid proportions, right-hand mace, glowing chest rune, and readable orange eyes.
Input image: gameplay blockout used only as proportion, silhouette and weapon-hand reference; do not copy primitive geometry or UI.
Subject: an ancient undead dungeon sentinel assembled from pale aged ossuary bone and restrained oxidized dark-green bronze armor. Exposed skull-like head with large readable eye sockets glowing ember orange. Broad armored ribcage with one central diamond-shaped teal memory rune. Strong shoulders, clear elbows and knees, sturdy feet. Right hand carries a one-handed ceremonial mace with a long dark wood/bronze grip and a compact faceted bronze head.
Style/medium: polished stylized dark-fantasy game character concept art with believable PBR material definition, strong low-poly-friendly forms, clean hard-surface shapes, subtle hand-painted wear, suitable for AI-assisted 3D reconstruction.
Composition/framing: wide landscape sheet. Center full-body three-quarter hero holding the mace. Consistent front, side and back neutral views plus head, chest rune, weapon and material insets. Entire character visible with generous spacing.
Lighting/mood: neutral studio lighting; dark warm-gray background; soft rim light; teal rune and orange eyes as the only emissive accents.
Color palette: ivory aged bone, charcoal and oxidized dark-teal bronze, muted brown leather/wood, teal rune and ember-orange eyes.
Materials/textures: chipped but not noisy bone, hammered bronze, worn leather wrap, modest edge wear and grime concentrated in cavities.
Constraints: rig-friendly humanoid anatomy; joints visually separable; no robes, capes, dangling chains, loose cloth, floating armor, tiny fragile spikes, excessive ornaments, gore, text, labels, logos, trademarks, or watermark. Keep the silhouette readable at Quest resolution and preserve the blockout's gameplay proportions and mace reach.
```

## Turnaround técnico

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: technical orthographic character turnaround for AI-assisted 3D modeling
Primary request: Create a clean full-body turnaround of the exact same Ossuary Guardian design from the approved concept reference.
Input image: concept v1 is the locked visual-design reference; preserve skull, anatomy, armor shapes, palette, emissives, proportions, boots, gauntlets and design landmarks exactly.
Composition/framing: wide landscape sheet with exactly three equal-scale full-body orthographic views: front, left side, and back. Neutral symmetrical A-pose, arms slightly away from torso, straight legs and aligned feet. Entire skull, hands and feet visible with generous margins. Same mace separately at far right in strict side orthographic view.
Style/medium: polished neutral game-production model sheet with crisp silhouette and clear joint separation.
Lighting/mood: flat neutral studio lighting with minimal shadows on a medium-dark gray background.
Constraints: exact same character in every view; identical scale and alignment; rig-friendly; no perspective distortion, hero pose, close-ups, material swatches, pose variation, cropped parts, extra limbs, floating armor, cloth, chains, gore, text, labels, measurement marks, logos, trademarks, or watermark.
```
